## 🌟 Project Overview

**Unknown Beyond** is a curated anomaly intelligence platform that bridges the gap between conspiracy forums and rigid academic archives. We maintain analytical discipline while fostering evidence-based discourse about phenomena that challenge our understanding.

### Mission Statement

To create a structured, credible, and community-driven platform for investigating:
- 🛸 UAP (Unidentified Aerial Phenomena) sightings
- 👽 ET (Extraterrestrial) encounters
- 🏛️ Ancient civilizations and mysteries
- 🌌 Deep space anomalies
- 🔬 Theoretical physics and fringe science
- 📜 Government disclosures and declassified documents

## 🎯 Credibility Framework

All content is classified using a transparent 5-level system:

1. **Verified** 🟢 - Multiple official sources, documented evidence
2. **High Credibility** 🔵 - Credible witnesses, supporting data
3. **Medium Credibility** 🟡 - Reasonable evidence with gaps
4. **Contested** 🟠 - Disputed evidence, conflicting accounts
5. **Speculative** ⚪ - Theoretical, minimal evidence

## 🔒 Content Integrity Policy

- Clear separation of Verified, Contested, and Speculative content
- Mandatory source citation
- Evidence type classification
- No defamatory claims
- No medical misinformation
- Moderation guidelines enforced
