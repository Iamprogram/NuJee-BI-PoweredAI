About Unknown Beyond

Bridging curiosity and analytical rigor in the exploration of unexplained phenomena

Our Mission

Unknown Beyond exists to create a structured, credible, and evidence-augmented platform for exploring extraterrestrial research, unidentified aerial phenomena, and the unexplained mysteries of our universe.

We position ourselves between pure conspiracy forums (which lack credibility) and rigid academic portals (which can be too restrictive). Our goal is to maintain analytical discipline while fostering open-minded, evidence-based discourse.

What We Are

  • A curated anomaly intelligence platform
  • Evidence-based and source-critical
  • Open to unexplained phenomena
  • Committed to responsible discourse

What We Are Not

  • A conspiracy theory echo chamber
  • A platform for sensationalism
  • Dismissive of genuine curiosity
  • A source of defamatory or harmful content

Credibility Framework

All content on Unknown Beyond is classified using our transparent credibility system:

Verified

Information confirmed by multiple reliable sources, official documents, or peer-reviewed research. Highest level of confidence.

Example: Pentagon-confirmed UAP videos, declassified government documents

High Credibility

Evidence from credible sources (military personnel, scientists, officials) with supporting documentation but lacking independent verification.

Example: Military pilot testimonies, radar data from official sources

Medium Credibility

Reasonable evidence with some corroboration but significant gaps or conflicting accounts. Requires further investigation.

Example: Multiple witness accounts without physical evidence

Contested

Claims with disputed evidence, conflicting expert opinions, or alternative explanations. Significant debate exists.

Example: Historical accounts with limited documentation

Speculative

Theoretical concepts, hypotheses, or claims lacking substantial evidence. Presented for consideration and discussion.

Example: Theoretical physics concepts, unverified claims

Important Disclaimer

Unknown Beyond explores unexplained phenomena and encourages critical thinking. The content on this platform varies in credibility and should be independently verified.

  • We do not make definitive claims about the nature or origin of unexplained phenomena
  • All content is clearly labeled with credibility ratings
  • We encourage scientific skepticism and evidence-based analysis
  • User-contributed content does not represent the views of Unknown Beyond
  • This platform is for informational and educational purposes only

We maintain a strict policy against defamatory content, medical claims, or dangerous misinformation. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team.

Our Approach

Evidence-First

Every case requires documented evidence. We prioritize radar data, videos, official documents, and credible witness testimony.

Community-Driven

Our global community of researchers, analysts, and enthusiasts contributes analysis and investigation while maintaining standards.

Source Critical

We evaluate sources carefully, considering credibility, bias, and verification. Transparency is mandatory.

Analytical Rigor

We apply scientific thinking and logical analysis while remaining open to possibilities that challenge current understanding.

The Team

Unknown Beyond is maintained by a dedicated team of researchers, data analysts, and developers passionate about understanding unexplained phenomena through rigorous investigation.
