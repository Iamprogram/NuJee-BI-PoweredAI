Credibility Framework

  • Verified: Multiple reliable sources.
  • High Credibility: Strong evidence, limited independent confirmation.
  • Medium: Partial corroboration, notable gaps.
  • Contested: Conflicting expert views.
  • Speculative: Hypotheses with little direct evidence.
