Chapter 01

Prelude: The Bright Age

Cities shimmered beneath a calm sky, and the horizon promised endless growth. ReckonLight documents the final era of confidence before the first systems began to fail.

Chapter 02

Collapse: The Quiet Unraveling

Infrastructure degraded in silence, then all at once. Networks flickered, oceans advanced, and the world learned how quickly certainty can become memory.

Chapter 03

Afterglow: Life Beyond Certainty

Communities adapted through shared knowledge, salvage engineering, and ritual. In the fading light, culture became the last renewable energy source.

Chapter 04

Legacy: What Survives the Night

The site closes with a question rather than an ending: if history is fragile, what should be preserved first when the next dawn is uncertain?
